## Ships

Pages live in Spaces. Each Space has its own logins, tint and history, and everything comes back
when you relaunch. Install and update with Homebrew.

## Done when

- [ ] Two Spaces signed in to the same site as two different accounts stay separate across a relaunch.
- [ ] Quitting and relaunching restores every Space and page, and loads only the visible page.
- [ ] The launcher finds a page from history by typing part of its title.
- [ ] `brew install --cask` installs the app.

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- Tiling, the vim layer, and the page tree. History is a flat list of visits here.
- Syncing Spaces between machines.
