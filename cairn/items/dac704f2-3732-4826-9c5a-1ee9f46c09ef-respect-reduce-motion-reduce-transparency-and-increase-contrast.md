## Problem

Glass and motion are part of the look, and they must yield to the system settings.

## Proposal

Reduce Motion replaces slides and springs with fades or nothing. Reduce Transparency replaces glass
with opaque theme colours. Increase Contrast strengthens hairlines and text.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] Each setting changes the UI live, verified against a checklist of every animated or glass surface.
