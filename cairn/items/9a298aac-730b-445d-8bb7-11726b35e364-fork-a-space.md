## Problem

Sometimes you want to try something without disturbing a Space's pages, like a git worktree for
browsing.

## Proposal

"Fork Space" copies the Space's pages and layout into a new Space that shares its jar. "Fork Space
Fresh" gives the copy an empty jar.

## Acceptance criteria

- [ ] A fork shares logins with its source, and a fresh fork does not.
- [ ] A fork's history starts empty and is recorded separately.
