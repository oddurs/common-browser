## Ships

Pages tile side by side within a Space, and everything can be driven from the keyboard, including
link hints and an optional vim layer. History keeps the branches you backed out of.

## Done when

- [ ] Split, focus, swap and close tiles without touching the mouse.
- [ ] With `keys.mode = "vim"`, a page can be read, followed and closed with no modifier keys, and typing in a text field is never intercepted.
- [ ] Going back and then following a different link keeps both branches, and both are reachable.

## Explicitly not in this milestone

- Custom key bindings beyond choosing `standard` or `vim`.
- Floating or free-form windows inside a Space.
